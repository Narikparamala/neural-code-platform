How each piece works

Newsletter & stories are delivered by FormSubmit to the project inbox — no database, no subscription fee. The first submission triggers a one-time activation email; click it once and every later submission lands automatically.
Community accounts are not enabled yet. Real authentication needs a free Supabase project (or similar); until that exists, the Telegram channel is the community hub and stories are moderated through this console.
